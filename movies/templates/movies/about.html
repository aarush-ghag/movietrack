About MovieTrack

MovieTrack is a web application for organizing and tracking the movies you watch.

Users can maintain a personal watchlist, mark movies as watched, give movies ratings, write personal reviews, and organize movies into custom lists.

MovieTrack was created as a CSCI 565 final project by Aarush Ghag and William Nitto.
